💡 How to Use

  1. Set a Goal Name: What do you want to save for? (e.g., Vacation, New Phone)
  2. Set Target Amount: How much money do you want to save?
  3. Click "Add Goal": Your goal will appear below with a progress bar
  4. Add Money to Goal: Click the 💰 "Add Money" button to save towards it
  5. Track Progress: Watch the progress bar fill up as you get closer to your target
  6. Delete Goal: Click the ✕ button on any goal to remove it

💭 Tips: The progress bar automatically caps at 100% when you reach your target. Create multiple goals for different savings targets!
